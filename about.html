About TRACE

Two rovers go in first, so rescuers know what is safe before they follow.

The problem

Rescuers shouldn’t enter blind.

When an earthquake, fire or flood hits, rescue teams have to enter damaged buildings without knowing which routes are safe.

They can’t see structural hazards or which zones are already cleared. That uncertainty puts lives at risk, and most of it is avoidable.

Our solution

Two rovers go in first.

TRACE is an offline, affordable, two-rover system that scouts high-risk indoor spaces and maps hazards in real time.

Two identical rovers inspect accessible areas side by side and send what they see to a single local command post.

Sensors, cameras and our own low-cost mapping technology capture conditions inside. Our software merges everything into one clear picture, shared online with responders and civilians.

Key features

What TRACE does.

Real-time hazard mapping

Hazards and cleared zones show up on one map as the rovers move through the building.

Mapping

Autonomous navigation with manual override

The rovers navigate on their own, and an operator can take control at any time.

Control
