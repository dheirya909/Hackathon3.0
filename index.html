TRACE

Tactical Reconnaissance and Assessment for Critical Environments.

FIG 1.0 — FIELD ROVER
WHAT WE DO

Disaster response, mapped in real time.

TRACE is a disaster-response system built around two intelligent rovers designed to enter dangerous or inaccessible areas where human responders may not know where it is safe to go. Equipped with multiple sensors, cameras, and our own low-cost mapping technology, the rovers explore the environment and collect critical data such as terrain conditions and other real-time information. Our custom software processes and combines this data to create a clear picture of the affected area and can be downloaded directly from our website. The collected information is transmitted to our online platform, where civilians and response teams can access real-time disaster updates and situational information.
