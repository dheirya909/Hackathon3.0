01 // What we do

Disaster response, mapped in real time.

TRACE is a disaster-response system built around two intelligent rovers that enter dangerous or inaccessible areas where responders can’t tell what’s safe.

Sensors, cameras and our own low-cost mapping technology capture terrain conditions and live data from inside the zone.

Custom software merges it into one clear picture, shared on an online platform for civilians and response teams.
